How selling works

STEP 1Upload

Your asset register and a few photos. You get the range on this page.

STEP 2Request inspection

Send the lot below. We confirm pickup location and schedule an inspector.

STEP 3Independent inspection

Every device is power-tested and graded A–D. You approve the reserve price.

STEP 4Live auction

Vetted ITADs, refurbishers and authorised recyclers bid per sub-lot.

STEP 5Pickup & certificates

Paid before pickup. Data-wipe certificates and EPR paperwork follow.
