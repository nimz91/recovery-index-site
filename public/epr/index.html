    How the numbers are built

    Schedule III target = the year's target % × tonnes you placed on the market one average life earlier (year Y − X). Targets: 60% in FY 2023-24 and 2024-25, 70% in FY 2025-26 and 2026-27, 80% from FY 2027-28. Certificates trade between 30% (floor) and 100% (ceiling) of the environmental compensation (EC) rate for the category.
