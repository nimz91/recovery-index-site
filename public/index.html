Method and sources

Live prices. Metal prices and USD/INR update every 2 hours from 9 am to 9 pm IST, Monday to Friday, from a commercial data feed. Each table shows when its prices were last updated.

RI Secondary Metals Index. Each grade's price is the midpoint of the Delhi wholesale buy and sell rate, observed daily. A metal's sub-index is the average of its grades' prices relative to 14 Oct 2026, × 100; the composite weights copper 35%, aluminium 25%, brass 15%, lead 15%, zinc 10%. Between daily observations, the live estimate moves each grade with its metal's live price in rupees (brass with 65% copper, 35% zinc).

RI E-waste Index. Each item starts from an observed dealer buying rate and then moves with the metals it contains, in rupees: price now = observed rate × (Σ share of value from each metal × that metal's change since the observation + the share that tracks no metal). The index is the equal-weighted average of the items relative to 14 Oct 2026, × 100. Value shares are modelled estimates.

RI Plastics Index. Each recycled grade starts from an observed Indian market rate (marketplace median asking rates and dealer ranges) and moves with its virgin polymer's price: price now = observed rate × (80% × virgin price change since the observation + 20% fixed for collection and processing). Virgin prices are trade ranges for PET, HDPE, PP, LDPE and ABS. The index is the equal-weighted average of the grades relative to 14 Oct 2026, × 100. Plastics prices update when the virgin references change, typically every one to two weeks.

Other prices. Base metals: LME 3-month. Precious metals: spot. Battery metals: GFEX lithium carbonate and LME cobalt. Black mass: quarterly payables. India scrap and e-waste observations come from dealer boards (Delhi wholesale, Jalna, MetalMandi) and are indicative, excluding 18% GST, not offers. Sources are credited and linked in each table.

Prices are for information only and are not trading or investment advice.
